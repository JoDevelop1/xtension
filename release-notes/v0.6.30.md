# Xtension v0.6.30

This release makes the optional AI setup clear and approachable while retaining the Chrome Web Store privacy safeguards.

- One simple **Enable AI features** switch replaces the previous legalistic disclosure checkbox and the redundant second enable toggle.
- The interface now says plainly that the user sends selected content directly to OpenAI with their ChatGPT account through the local open-source connector. JoDevelop receives and stores none of the content, request, or response.
- All user-facing writing, image, activation, connector, account, options, and error flows are localized in English, French, German, Spanish, and Japanese.
- PDF export and X/Twitter relationship badges remain local and independent of AI.
