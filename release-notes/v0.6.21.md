# Xtension v0.6.21

This interface release makes X/Twitter's inline relationship and reply controls shorter and easier to read.

Accounts you follow still show a blue **✓ Following** badge. Accounts you do not follow now show the same compact label with a red **✕**, while the tooltip and accessible label retain the complete positive or negative meaning. The inline **Reply** button is now green, making it visually distinct from both relationship states.

The exact `Docteur Laurent Alexandre` author row reported from the home timeline is covered by a real-browser layout harness. The name, relationship badge, and **Reply** button render on one line with identical vertical centers.
