# Xtension v0.6.19

Xtension now makes the X/Twitter relationship state visible directly in the timeline. Authors whose relationship data is already present in X's timeline response receive a clear **Following** or **Not following** badge, without opening their hover card or making one request per profile.

AI-assisted replies now also work on Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, Bluesky, and YouTube. A shared toolbar offers correction, translation, generation, and contextual suggested replies beside supported reply editors. Prompts adapt to the active platform and generated text is inserted into the editor for review.

Xtension never presses the final publish or submit control. The user remains responsible for reviewing and sending every reply.
